# WIL-PORTFOLIO-2025
My WIL-PORTFOLIO-2025 that consists of all the work I have done so far for the year contained in 5 categories:

1. Business Communication

Situation: During the work readiness training, I had to demonstrate professional business communication through minute writing and report preparation for the work done for month.

Task: My task was to write clear, professional, and well-structured messages suitable for workplace standards.

Action: I drafted minutes from the recording and reports for the work done.

Result: I improved my ability to communicate formally and concisely. I now feel more confident in writing professional documents and responding appropriately in workplace communication.


2. Interview Skills

Situation: I participated in an interview preparation exercise as part of the work readiness program.

Task: I had to prepare answers to common interview questions and present myself in a professional manner.

Action: I researched potential interview questions, practiced responses using the STAR method, and rehearsed.

Result: I became more comfortable with structuring answers, improved my body language, and learned how to highlight my skills effectively in an interview setting.



3. Mock Interview

Situation: I took part in a formal mock interview to simulate a real-world job application process.

Task: The task was to respond to interview questions while demonstrating professionalism, confidence, and preparation.

Action: I dressed appropriately, I recorded the interview and answered questions.

Result: I believe this was a stepping stone for what is to come as within the interview it demonstrated who I am.




4. Professional Networking

Situation: Networking was introduced as an important skill for career growth and opportunity finding.

Task: I was tasked with creating a professional online presence and connecting with relevant people in my group and the work we did and the issues we encountered.

Action: I created/updated my MS teams and LinkedIn profile so that I could communicate and work better with my team.

Result: I successfully built system with my team due these platforms the work that was put in place.




5. Workplace Etiquette

Situation: Workplace etiquette was covered in training to prepare us for professional behavior in different workplace settings.

Task: I needed to understand and apply workplace etiquette, including punctuality, respect, teamwork, and help doing the sprints on Trello

Action: I participated in group discussions, helped with the minutes, the sprints, backlog and the programming of the project itself.

Result: I developed a stronger awareness of professional conduct, improved my teamwork skills, and can now confidently demonstrate appropriate workplace etiquette in real work environments.
